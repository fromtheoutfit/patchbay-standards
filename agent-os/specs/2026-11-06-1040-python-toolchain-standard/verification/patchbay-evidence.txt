### 5. uv_build: which backend uv 0.13.7 builds with, by [build-system] range

uv_build requirement" (https://docs.astral.sh/uv/concepts/build-backend/). Its source at the 0.13.7 tag

pin names another version; otherwise uv builds through PEP 517 with a uv_build package from the index.
